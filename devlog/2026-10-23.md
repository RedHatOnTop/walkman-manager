## The update ends by compiling the apps (19:0x-20:0x)

An update keeps the odex PackageManager compiled into `/data`. A kit build keeps the GSI's
fingerprint, so to Android the update is no OTA and nothing compiles them again before the
next idle charge. Kit 80ee792 showed what that costs: until `bg-dexopt-job` ran, Settings,
microG and PermissionController still loaded odex that named the old library path.

  read-back still ends as done.
- In the app on Xvfb (binary 1ee0f53e…, the e2e data folder): the Studio built kit 80ee792 from
  its published archive in 3:15, 27 steps. Against the hand build the manifest differs only in

  boot, read-back (slot b, kernel, 4/4 files), `bg-dexopt-job` "Success" in 9 s (the hand run
  at 18:34 had already compiled the ones that needed it). The page lists nine stages ending in
  Optimize apps, all done, "Installed".
- Afterwards, four cold starts of Settings took 926-1055 ms, and the app image was mapped.
- Three warnings remain after each boot, from microG's companion, Aurora Store and Firefox. Their

## The unlock stage against a fake fastboot

The Unlock stage has never run from the app: the ZX507 was unlocked by hand on 10-14 and is
not going to be locked again to try it. Its loop moved from the app's install job into
`Fastboot::unlock` in uwm-core, with tests that run it against a fastboot script playing six
bootloaders: one that answers OKAY at once, one silent past the command's timeout, one lost on
USB mid-erase (`FAILED (Status read failed (No such device))`) and polled until it reads yes,
one that refuses (`FAILED (remote: 'Flashing Unlock is not allowed')`), one that says OKAY and
stays locked, and one that never answers. Two things changed on the way:

- A refusal used to be polled for the whole 25 minutes before the stage failed, since only a
  successful command stopped the polling. A bootloader that answered with `FAILED (remote: …)`
  has nothing to erase, so its first reading now ends the stage.
- A command that took the whole budget left no time to read the switch back, and the stage
  failed even if the player had unlocked. The switch is now read at least once.
