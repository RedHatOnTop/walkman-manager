## Alpha, not a stable release

- The owner: the A55's Android is alpha firmware with no stability promised, and the app
  is not a stable release either; a release goes out with that said, and what is left is
  fixed after it.
- `public/README.md`: the status line reads "Alpha: not a stable release", names the
  NW-A50 series' Android alpha (walkman-a55) and the NW-ZX500's beta (walkman-zx507, the
  owner's call), and points at the GPL's no-warranty sections.
- The sidebar shows an `ALPHA` / `알파` tag beside `ULTIMATE`, outlined in `--warn`, with the
  sentence in both languages as its tooltip. Seen in the browser preview at 1280×800, Korean
  on the dark theme and English on the light one: the tag sits on the `ULTIMATE` line and the
  brand still fits the sidebar.
- walkman-a55 says the same in its README and install guide, walkman-zx507 2db7296 says
  beta in its own. Neither kit changed for it, so the pins stayed; the A50 pin moved later
  that day, to walkman-a55's fresh root be2268d.

## Beta, with the kits

- The owner, later the same day (relayed by the session that runs the Walkman pages on
  personnya.dev): the app goes out as beta, released together with the kits, not as alpha.
- `public/README.md` reads "Beta: not a stable release"; the sidebar tag and its tooltip say
  beta in both languages. The kits keep their own labels: the NW-A50 series' Android alpha,
  the NW-ZX500's beta.

## The end of a build, in front

- The owner: a finished build should say so in a large window or turn the button into
  Install, because the build list sits far down the Studio.
- PR #16: a build that ends opens a large sheet over whatever page is open, with the
  player, the filled step strip, kit, type or firmware key, time, size, and Install. A
  failed build names the step it stopped at and shows the log tail. A kit with a finished
  set offers Install, and Build becomes Build again. Run for real with the A50 kit on Xvfb:
  built from the Studio, waited on Players, and the sheet came up there after 0:46.

## The pages as a spec sheet

- The owner: the interface still read as AI-made. The first pages were redone in the print
  `docs/design.md` describes and shown in a demo artifact, which the owner accepted once
  the brand block left the sidebar.
- PR #17: the device page and Settings are sections down the page (hairline, name in a
  210 px column, rows at the right) instead of a tile grid. Choices are underlined words,
  tags are plain words, buttons are square-cornered, and the "model by storage" remark is a
  footnote. The sidebar has no brand block, and the stage word ("Beta" after #15) sits by
  the version. The Studio, Library, Home and install page keep their look for now.
